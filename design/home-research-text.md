I am a PhD candidate studying how the human brain learns motor skills and how learning changes the brain at the cellular level. In my lab we use functional and diffusion MRI to study the hippocampus, a structure long tied to declarative memory, during motor sequence learning. Our work has shown that hippocampal activity during short rests predicts performance gains, that hippocampal connectivity changes transiently within 30 minutes of learning, and that learning produces both a short-lived expansion of cell bodies and a sustained increase in cell-process density in key motor regions.
